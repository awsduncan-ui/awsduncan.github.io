What families can check before visiting

Weston, Honiton EX14 3NZ, UK

Traditional Devon pub dating back to the 1700s, situated on the River Otter near Honiton with a spacious beer garden and children's play area. The pub serves cask ales including local Otter Ales, offers a restaurant menu with daily specials and a popular Sunday carvery, and welcomes families and dogs.
