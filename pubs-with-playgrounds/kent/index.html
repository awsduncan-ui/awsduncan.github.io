A practical family-pub shortlist

Kent’s family-pub options range from village gardens with climbing frames to larger venues with dedicated play facilities. This checked list makes the distinction visible before you choose where to eat.

Coverage: Maidstone, Medway, Sevenoaks and central Kent, using an approximately 35 km guide radius from Maidstone. Border areas can overlap neighbouring guides.

How these listings are checked

Pages are generated from the same approved public feed used by the app. Only human- or community-verified venues that are not marked permanently closed are included.

Play equipment, menus and opening arrangements can change. We show the last recorded check on every card and link to the venue where available. The public feed was last updated 10 August 2026.

Spotted a change? Email hello@pubswithplaygrounds.com with the pub name and town. We do not read the private Firestore /pubs collection from this website.
