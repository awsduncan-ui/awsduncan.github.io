A practical family-pub shortlist

Finding outdoor space in and around London can take more planning than the pub lunch itself. This guide brings together checked venues where children have a real playground, indoor play area or soft play—not merely a children’s menu.

Coverage: Greater London and nearby areas around the M25, using an approximately 30 km guide radius from central London. Border areas can overlap neighbouring guides.

How these listings are checked

Pages are generated from the same approved public feed used by the app. Only human- or community-verified venues that are not marked permanently closed are included.

Play equipment, menus and opening arrangements can change. We show the last recorded check on every card and link to the venue where available. The public feed was last updated 31 July 2026.

Spotted a change? Email hello@pubswithplaygrounds.com with the pub name and town. We do not read the private Firestore /pubs collection from this website.
