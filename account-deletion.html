Account Deletion

You can delete your Pubs With Playgrounds account directly in the app from the Account screen.

What deletion removes

  • Your user account
  • Your favourites
  • Your reviews
  • Saved personal profile data linked to your account

What may be retained

Some public venue contributions may be retained in anonymised form where needed to preserve venue information, maintain community content, or comply with legal, fraud, safety, or record-keeping obligations.
