What families can check before visiting

52 Station Rd, Gomshall, Guildford GU5 9LB, UK

This Grade II listed 11th-century mill, converted to a pub in 2010 and fully refurbished in 2022, sits beside the River Tillingbourne in rural Gomshall near Guildford. It features a children's play area, riverside garden and patio, seasonal British dishes with gluten-free options, and cask ales.
