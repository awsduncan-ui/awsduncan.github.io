What families can check before visiting

123 Main Rd, Biggin Hill, Westerham TN16 3DX

Historic pub in Biggin Hill operating since 1908, serving quality ales including local Westerham Brewery beers alongside Indian thali and classic mains. Features a children's play area, spacious garden with covered sections, pool table, and free parking; live music and entertainment feature regularly.
