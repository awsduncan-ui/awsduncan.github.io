Know before you go

See exactly what you're walking into.

Every listing shows the things that decide a family day out — the actual play equipment, whether there's a beer garden, a children's menu, baby change and parking — with real photos and honest details, not vague “family friendly” tags.

  • ✓Real photos of the play area, not stock images
  • ✓Opening hours, food times and facilities at a glance
  • ✓Distance and directions from wherever you are

What's listed

The details that make or break a family lunch.

“Family friendly” can mean anything. Pubs With Playgrounds tells you exactly what's there — from the climbing frame in the garden to whether you'll get the pram through the door.

Listings are checked against available evidence from pub websites, photos and parent reports. The database is a work in progress, so parents and pubs are encouraged to flag anything that has changed.
