About Pubs With Playgrounds

Pubs With Playgrounds is a free UK pub finder helping parents find pubs and hotels with real play areas and useful family facilities.

Why it exists

It was started by Alastair Duncan, a dad of two, to make it easier to find pub lunches that work for adults and children.

How listings are built

Listings combine pub websites, Google place information, parent reports, photos and direct corrections from pubs where available.

Details can change, so pubs and parents are welcome to send a correction when something is missing or out of date.
